# MyHQ - The Universal Strategy Engine
MyHQ is a dynamic blueprinting tool for gamers and developers that enables the active planning of game strategy 
in a controlled and familiar environment. Users learn how to interact with our tools once, and then they can use 
our tool for any number of games-- even including tools to add games into the options one can design for, leading 
to endless possibilities!

For the scope of COS229-O & COS239-O "Project & Portfolio 2 & 3", the focus is on the core functionality and key 
technology to prove that the idea serves a novel purpose. Functionality is intended to only include a landing page 
and the start of the designer tool, to begin initial grid development and testing.

## Transition MyHQ from concept into a functional Proof-of-Concept WebApp.
The concept behind MyHQ was created as Steve Asphodel's Thesis for the Master's of Science in Innovation & 
Entreprenuership during 2022, after exploring the limited availability of planning tools-- less than a handful 
exist both then and now. Rather than try to cater to one or a handful of games, MyHQ aims to jump to the finish 
line by delivering a system that can cater to all games, regardless of format or data.

In SDV3012-O "Applied Human-Computer Interactions", team members Steve, Nelson, and Albert redefined the 
visual design and the overall concept behind MyHQ. The baseline has been defined in full detail, and the stages 
of development are planned to enable Agile workflow!

## Course Scope
There are 3 key project goals for the current Backlog A scope of our team:
1. Simple onboarding to explain app purpose and guide user to trying the tool.
2. Background tool loading for an optimal jump-to-start functionality.
3. A grid design tool featuring a toolbar of pre-made shapes, that can be added or removed from the grid.

Our stretch goals explore some of the more complex parts of the project:
1. The ability to save and load layouts, initially to the device as JSON, then with a database.
2. Developing the form system to implement game Schema for dynamic project designs.
3. Iterating off of the Toolbar of Backlog A and the Schema Form of Backlog B, implement a Shape Designer.

Wherever the project leaves off in Agile Cycle 1, Agile Cycle 2 will carry on from that point to refine existing
systems. Once a functional designer and onboarding process is established, the project will move towards Alpha 
Deployment and Business Startup.
